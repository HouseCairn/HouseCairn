# Install on a phone

HouseCairn installs on a phone's home screen like an app: it opens in its own window,
without the address bar, and Today and the task list of your home stay readable without
a connection. The instance must be served over HTTPS.

## Android

In Chrome, Edge or Firefox, open the address of your instance and sign in. The browser
offers **Install app** (Chrome and Edge, at the bottom of the screen or in the ⋮ menu) or
**Install** (Firefox, in the ⋮ menu). HouseCairn then appears with the other apps.

## iPhone and iPad

In Safari, open the address of your instance and sign in. Tap **Share**, then **Add to
Home Screen**, then **Add**. The name under the icon is the name of the instance.

## Without a connection

Today and the task list of your home are kept on the phone each time you open them with a
connection. Without one, they show as they were then, under the banner **Offline: data
from** followed by the date and time; the buttons that change something, **Done** and the
forms, are off until the connection is back. Nothing is saved for later. Any other page
shows that it was not saved.

What is kept belongs to you alone: signing out deletes it, and so does another person
signing in on the same phone. The other homes are never kept, only the one you opened last.
