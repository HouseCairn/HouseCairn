# Item

One piece of equipment or one surface (a wall, a floor), at
`/h/<home id>/items/<item id>/`.

The page shows what you noted: brand and model, serial number, installation
date, the warranty (a label tells whether it is still running), the maintenance
contract with its link, notes, then the links and the photos of the item. Only
what is filled in is shown. **Edit the item** opens the [item form](item-form.md).

The maintenance log and the cost of ownership of an item arrive with the tasks:
there is no tab for them yet.

**Delete the item** (in the edit form) asks for confirmation. Members and owners
change the item; read-only members see it all and change nothing.
