# Compare projects

Side by side, at `/h/<home id>/projects/compare/?p=<project id>&p=<project id>`, for the
projects ticked in the [list](projects.md) (at most six). A table gives for each project its
status, its **planned cost** (the total of its planned budget lines), what is **committed**
by accepted quotes, its estimated duration, its priority and its preferred season, in the
currency of the home. The title of a project opens it. Projects of another home are never
shown, whatever the address says. With fewer than two projects, the page asks you to tick
more.
