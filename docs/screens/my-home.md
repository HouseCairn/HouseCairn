# My home

What concerns you, at `/me/`; **Home** in the navigation opens it once you are
signed in. With several homes, the selector at the top chooses one, or **All my
homes** to see them together.

For each home, under its name (with links to [Today](today.md), the
[task list](tasks.md) and the [history](history.md)):

- **Assigned to me**: the open tasks that are yours, the late ones first.
- **Overdue in the home**: what is late for other people or for nobody.
- **Nobody has taken these**: open tasks with nobody in charge that are not late.
- **Warranties ending soon**: items whose warranty ends within 60 days; a row
  opens the item.

The lists share no row, so a late task of yours is only in the first one. Each list
shows at most 20 rows. A row opens the [task](task.md); close it from
[Today](today.md). A home with nothing for you says so.

**Display preferences** at the bottom opens the [display preferences](display-preferences.md).
