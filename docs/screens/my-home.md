# Today in several homes

What concerns you across your homes, at the root address `/`, when you can open
more than one home and chose **All homes** in the selector of the app bar (it is also
what `/` shows before you choose a home). With one home, `/` opens the
[Today](today.md) of that home; with none, it shows the [start page](start-page.md).
The address `/me/` of earlier versions redirects to `/`.

The tabs are **Today** and **Calendar** (the [calendar](calendar.md) of all your
homes). Each home has its name as a heading, which opens its
[Today](today.md), and **Report a problem** under it, which opens the
[form](report-form.md) for that home. Under them:

- **Assigned to me**: the open tasks that are yours, the late ones first.
- **Overdue in the home**: what is late for other people or for nobody.
- **Nobody has taken these**: open tasks with nobody in charge that are not late.
- **Warranties ending soon**: items whose warranty ends within 60 days; a row
  opens the item.

The lists share no row, so a late task of yours is only in the first one. Each row
names its home. Each list shows at most 20 rows. A row opens the [task](task.md); close it from
[Today](today.md). A home with nothing for you says so.

**Display preferences** at the bottom opens the [display preferences](display-preferences.md).

## The current home

From two homes on, the app bar shows the name of the current home with a selector
(on a computer, above the column of sections). The sections open on that home:
**Rooms**, **Projects**, **Budget**, and **Today** unless you chose **All homes**.
The current home is the one you last chose in the selector, or the home of the last
page of a home you opened; before either, the first of your homes by name.

- Choosing a home opens the same screen in that home (the room list, the budget), or
  the first screen of the section when the page was a record of the other home (a
  room, a task).
- **All homes** is offered on Today only: Today and the calendar then show all your
  homes together, and the other sections stay on the current home.
- The selector only lists the homes you belong to. A home you leave, or that is
  deleted, is replaced by another of your homes, or, with none left, by the page that
  offers to add one. With one home, there is no selector.
