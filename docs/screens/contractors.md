# Contractors

The people and companies who work in a home, at `/h/<home id>/contractors/` (the
**Contractors** tab of the [tasks](tasks.md) screens). Each row shows the name, the trade
and the phone number; a row opens the [sheet](contractor.md) of the contractor.
**Add a contractor** opens the [form](contractor-form.md).

Every member of the home reads the list; read-only members do not see the buttons that
change it. A contractor belongs to one home: another home cannot see it, even with its
address.
