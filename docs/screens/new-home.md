# Add a home

The form at `/h/new/`, opened by **Add a home** under the list of homes: a
name, the currency of the home's budget (an ISO 4217 code such as EUR) and its hemisphere
(**Northern** or **Southern**, for the seasons of the tasks).
You become the owner of the new home, and can then invite the rest of the
household.

**What the home has** lists common equipment: smoke alarms, a boiler, a fireplace,
mechanical ventilation, a hot water tank, gutters and so on. Each one ticked adds the
usual maintenance tasks of its [templates](task-templates.md) to the new home, in your
language, without a date; nothing ticked, no task. You change or delete them like any
other task, and add more later with **Add from a template**.
