# More

Everything that is not a daily gesture, at `/more/`, reached from the last
entry of the navigation bar on a phone. On a computer, More has no page of its
own: the same entries are unfolded in the column on the left, under the four
sections. The entries come in four groups:

- **Home**, about the home you are viewing (your only home, on the More page):
  its [settings](home.md) (name, currency, hemisphere, export),
  [Household and roles](household.md), [Reports](reports.md) (with the number of
  reports waiting, for the members who may write),
  [Contractors](contractors.md) and [QR labels](qr-labels.md). **My homes**
  lists your [homes](homes.md), to add one or open another.
- **Me**: your [profile](profile.md) (the name other people see), your
  [notifications](notifications.md), your
  [display preferences](display-preferences.md), the
  [calendar in another application](calendar.md) (secret feeds),
  [API tokens](api-tokens.md) (for Home Assistant or another tool) and
  [Security and sign-in](security-and-sign-in.md).
- **Instance**, for an administrator only:
  [instance administration](instance.md).
- **Help**: this documentation, the documentation of the API and the
  [About](about.md) page.

**Sign out** comes last. The actions that cannot be taken back, deleting your
account or a home, are not on More: they are at the bottom of their own page.
