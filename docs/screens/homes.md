# My homes

The list of the homes you belong to, at `/h/`. Each name opens the page of
that home; **Add a home** creates a new one, and **Restore a home** makes a new
home from the archive of **Export all data** (see
[Export and deletion](../administration/data.md)). A home that requires signing in
through a provider you did not use in this session is marked, for example
"Company SSO required".

The page **Restore a home** asks for the archive and how to restore it. **Restore**
brings the home back with its identifiers, and is refused while that home is on the
instance. **Restore as a copy** makes a new home next to it, with new identifiers and
its own copy of every file, under the name given in **Name of the copy** (left empty:
the name of the home followed by "(copy)"). The page says that the members and
invitations of the archive are not copied: you are the only member of the copy. With
script, **Name of the copy** shows only when the copy is chosen; without it, it always
shows and a restore ignores it. Back on **My homes**, a message names the home made.

The list is the entry **My homes** of [More](more.md), and its link at the top leads
back to More, on a phone. Opening a home that asks for a provider shows a page whose link
at the top leads back to **My homes**.
