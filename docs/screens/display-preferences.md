# Display preferences

How the [Today](today.md) screen is laid out for you, at `/me/preferences/`
(**More**, then **Display preferences**, or the **Display** link of Today). They
belong to you, for all your homes: the other people of a home keep their own.

- **Group by**: **Urgency** (today, then coming up), **Room**, **Person** or
  **Calendar** (one section per day).
- **Look ahead**: how far the coming section goes, from 1 week to 3 months.
- **Sections**: show or hide **Overdue**, **Coming up** and **In season**.

Until you save, the defaults apply: urgency, one week, every section. **Save**
brings you back to the page you came from. The same settings are in the
[API](../api/index.md).
