# Display preferences

How the [Today](today.md) screen is laid out for you, at `/me/preferences/`
(**More**, then **Display preferences**, in the **Me** group). They belong to you, for all your homes: the other people of a home keep their own.

- **Group by**: **Urgency** (today, then coming up), **Room**, **Person** or
  **Calendar** (one section per day).
- **Look ahead**: how far the coming section goes, from 1 week to 3 months.
- **Sections**: show or hide **Overdue**, **Coming up** and **In season**.
- **Language**: **Automatic** (your browser's language, then the default language of the
  instance) or one of the languages HouseCairn ships. Your choice wins over the language
  cookie, the browser and the default of the instance, on every device you sign in from,
  and applies from the next page. A call made with an API token keeps the language it asks
  for with `Accept-Language`.

Until you save, the defaults apply: urgency, one week, every section. **Save**
brings you back to the page you came from. The same settings are in the
[API](../api/index.md).
